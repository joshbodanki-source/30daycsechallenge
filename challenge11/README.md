Objective:

To develop a menu-driven C program to analyse seven days of water consumption across five campus zones, detect unusual usage, identify over-limit consumption, and recommend water conservation measures.

Algorithm:
1. Start the program.
2. Input and validate water consumption readings and daily targets for all five zones.
3. Display the water consumption data.
4. Calculate weekly total, average, highest usage and peak day for each zone.
5. Detect over-limit days and sudden usage spikes.
6. Calculate total excess water and unique alert days.
7. Rank the zones based on excess water consumption.
8. Generate suitable water conservation recommendations.
9. Display the menu until the user selects Exit.
10. Stop.
